Understand Nehemiah 2

Nehemiah 2:1–8 · About 3 minutes

30THE 30-SECOND BRIEF
Three things to understand before reading
  1. 1
    BEFORE THIS

    Babylon destroyed Jerusalem. Persia allowed the Jewish people to return, but the city’s walls were never rebuilt.

  2. 2
    RIGHT NOW

    The king notices Nehemiah’s sadness. Nehemiah has one opportunity to ask permission and support to rebuild Jerusalem.

  3. 3
    WHAT IS AT STAKE

    He needs imperial permission, protected travel and materials. The request carries personal and political risk.

People in this passage
When and where

About 445 BC in the Persian royal court. Jerusalem, roughly 1,200 km away, is the destination of Nehemiah’s request.

Persian rule

Judah was governed within the Persian Empire. Nehemiah therefore needed royal authority, official letters and resources.

Court culture

As cupbearer, Nehemiah held a trusted position close to the king. Visible sadness in the royal presence made this moment especially dangerous.

Why the walls mattered

Without walls, a city was vulnerable, commercially weak and publicly disgraced. Gates also supported trade and civic administration.
